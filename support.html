Help & Support

Protect your data first. If something goes wrong, do not delete the App; export a complete JSON backup whenever possible.

A bill cannot be saved

Make sure the amount is greater than zero and that the selected category matches expense or income. If an error appears, keep the full error message.

iCloud is not syncing

Confirm that the device is signed in to iCloud and that Smart Ledger can use iCloud. Under “Me → iCloud Sync,” tap “Check iCloud Status Again,” then choose Upload or Download.

Restore on a new device

On the old device, upload an iCloud backup and wait for “Sync Complete.” On the new device, sign in to the same Apple Account and then download the backup.

Before contacting support

Record the App version, iOS version, affected screen, steps to reproduce, and the complete error message. In the App, “Me → Privacy & Support → Feedback” can prepare basic diagnostic information.

一言记账

使用帮助

先保住数据，再处理问题。如遇异常，请不要删除 App，并优先导出 JSON 完整备份。

记一笔无法保存

确认金额大于 0，并为账单选择与支出或收入匹配的分类。如页面显示保存失败，请保留完整错误文字。

iCloud 没有同步

请确认设备已登录 iCloud，且已允许一言记账使用 iCloud。在“我的 → iCloud 同步”中先点“重新检查 iCloud 状态”，再选择上传或下载。

换机恢复

在旧设备上先上传 iCloud 备份，等待显示“同步完成”；然后在新设备上使用同一 Apple 账号登录 iCloud，再执行下载。

反馈前请准备

请记录 App 版本、iOS 版本、问题发生的页面、操作步骤与完整错误提示。可在 App 的“我的 → 隐私政策与支持 → 问题反馈”生成基本反馈信息。
